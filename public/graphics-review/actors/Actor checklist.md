# Actor comparisons

The 26 actors below were rendered from both Blender revisions in the same pose, from the same camera and in the same lighting. The galleries use neutral area lights for comparing shape and materials. Actual campaign lighting is shown in the gameplay captures.

## Pursuers

- **Bacteria** — Flared enamel hood, asymmetric spine and raised throat pleats.
- **Hound** — Longer double jaw, widened ears and raked muzzle cartilage.
- **Smiler** — Broader jaw with swept, raised cheek seams and heavier brows.
- **Deathmoth** — Scalloped wing hems and branching ribs in the membranes.
- **Skin-Stealer** — Slipped face, diagonal suture line and constricted rib cage.
- **Wretch** — Distended jaw, hunched vertebrae and recessed cheek folds.

## Masks and clothed figures

- **Kitty** — Elongated blind head and a nearly invisible rim around the face.
- **Watcher** — Peaked, mineral-rimmed face and a flared, uneven mantle.
- **Partygoer** — Slumped sack cheeks and seam folds beneath the painted smile.
- **Animation** — Carved cheek channels and more pronounced wooden jaw.
- **Animated King** — Uneven crown peaks and raised brass detailing across the mask.
- **Faceling** — Longer blank face, worn facial rim and flared coat shoulders.

## Limbs, water and machinery

- **Clump** — Asymmetric mass and raised branching cords across the knot.
- **Crawler** — Serrated back plates with chevron ridges and extended hooks.
- **Plastic Shark** — Taller dorsal fin, longer tail blade and raised gill louvers.
- **Leviathan** — Broader head, lowered lower jaw and an arcing gum ridge.
- **Security Camera** — Extended optical rain hood, narrow rim and recessed louvers.
- **Neighborhood Watch** — Flatter orbit with fleshy brow and lid folds.

## Regional creatures, residents and field suit

- **Aranea Membri** — Wider, keeled carapace with a raised medial seam and branching plates.
- **Scratcher** — Taller withers, longer rakes and three raised scars across the body.
- **Bone Thief** — Raised reach tendons, enlarged knuckles and longer hand silhouette.
- **Farmer Faceling** — Wider tilted brim, workwear patch and a larger motor housing.
- **Party Host** — Broader shoulder mass and a stepped, pleated chest.
- **Veil** — Tapered masks, branching porcelain fissures and irregular shroud hem.
- **Bellwether** — Flared chime petals, undulating edges and ringed clapper.
- **Field Explorer** — Structured shoulders, cinched midsection and raised chest harness.

## Export checks

Every skin retained its ordered joint list, and every weighted vertex held a normalized weight sum.
